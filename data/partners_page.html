    Our Sponsors + Partnerships

    On-Deck Alliance has been blessed by the Toledo Community and the Skateboarding Community through the founding of the non-profit, grants, event donations, and equipment donations to help us keep the "I Can" program running. See who we've teamed up with below. 

    The Frederick Douglass Community Association

    "The Doug" has donated in-kind facilities, staff support, and countless other resources to On-Deck Alliance that provide us with a space to work and learn and a valuable network of connections for students and ODA staffers. We would not be where we are today, serving the Toledo community, without the FDCA's support. Thank you!

    City of Toledo Youth Services

    The City of Toledo's Youth Programming Grant was pivotal in the development of the "I Can" Program with a donation of $15,000 in the summer of 2023. This grant allowed ODA to build a skatepark, purchase personal protective equipment for students, and pay staff. This grant has widened and strengthened the impact of On-Deck Alliance. Thank you, Toledo!

    The DUBS Foundation

    The DUBS Foundation is run by Austin and Nick Weber, who have been imperative team members since ODA's founding. The Webers build skate ramps and other skatable obstacles for class use and at the D.I.Y. park in Toledo. The Webers help create and support events that support the local skateboarding community as well as the "I Can" Program. 

    Austin Weber is the head coach, and he develops our curriculums and leads classes.

    Nick Weber serves as the Corporate Marketing Officer, establishing and maintaining relationships with our corporate sponsors. 

    Both of these gentlemen have been instrumental in the building of On-Deck Alliance and we could not do what we do without their help. Thank you, DUBS!

    Advocate Skateworks

    Travis at Advocate Skateworks has been an incredible resource in the development of the "I Can" Program through his relationships with notable skate companies, such as Vans. This relationship has helped ODA secure Vans skate shoes and an invaluable number of skateboard decks for students. Thank you Travis, and thank you ASW!

    Pizza Cat Toledo

    Pizza Cat of Toledo has supported ODA through a generous donation of THIRTY pizzas in the summer of 2024 for the 4 Cs Games fundraiser. Pizza Cat's mission aligns with On-Deck, with a core value being the goal of rebuilding the local community through the youth within the community. Thank you, Pizza Cat!

    Nuclear Skate Cult

    NSC is the premier skate team in the Toledo area. The team has supported our goals at On-Deck since day one through sponsoring and supporting events and fundraisers, recruiting and developing coaches and coaches' skills, promoting our shared mission to empower the youth of Toledo through skateboarding, and donating time and resources directly to our program. The gratefulness we have for NSC is immeasurable. Thank you, Nuclear Skate Cult! 

    Steez LLC

    Steez is a local skate company best known for its butter-smooth skate wax and stylish street apparel. Steez has supported ODA by donating prizes for events and contests, helping us in our mission to develop the youth in underserved areas of Toledo! Thank you, Steez!

    Lavish Hardware

    Lavish is another local skate company, best known for rock-solid hardware and unique apparel items. Lavish supports On-Deck with prizes for competitions and events, enabling the "I Can" Program to continue on its mission to help the youth of Toledo, Ohio. Thank you, Lavish!

    Modern Skate Shop

    Modern is a Detroit, Michigan-based skate shop and indoor skate park. Modern has contributed prizes for ODA events and fundraisers, helping to strengthen the skateboarding community in Toledo and helping On-Deck keep the "I Can" Program running. Thank you, Modern!

    Just Skateboarding

    Just Skateboarding is Toledo's local skate shop. Owner Scott Tutek provides not only skateboarding supplies, equipment, and apparel, but a wealth of knowledge and deep roots in the local skate scene. Thank you, Just, for your donation of prizes for ODA events!

    Ryno's Skate Shop

    Ryno's Skateboard Shop is a Fremont, Ohio-based skate shop. In addition to prizes for contests and fundraising events, Ryno's also strengthens On-Deck's connections to the Fremont skateboarding community, a connection we value deeply. Thank you, Ryno's!

    Lil Jawns Skateboard Rails

    Lil Jawns is a skater-owned, Texas-based company that supports skateboarding as a culture and as a community. With our aligned values and mission, Lil Jawns is an ideal partner for ODA. We are very grateful for the donation of prizes for fundraising events and contests. Thank you, Lil Jawns!

    Vans

    Vans is a well-known brand in the skate community, and On-Deck is blessed to have the support of Vans by the donation of skate shoes, tee shirts, and over 100 skateboard decks! Thank you, Vans, for recognizing and supporting On-Deck Alliance. 

    Printed on a Lark

    Printed on a Lark is a Toledo-based screen printing company. Emilia has donated her time, skillset, and resources to host a workshop to teach us at ODA how to use and maintain high-quality screen printing equipment. The ability to create tee shirts and other apparel has given us the opportunity to continue to raise funds to support our program, as well as providing a marketable skillset to teach to our students, and broadening our ability to serve our community. Thank you Emilia and Printed on a Lark!

    Smollie Skateboard Co.

     Smollie has generously donated training supplies used on a regular basis in our classroom. The Smollie Straps give students a safe and realistic experience skateboarding in our learning environment as they develop their skills. Thank you, Smollie!

    Keen Ramps

    Keen Ramps is a Long Beach, California-based skate company that has donated a ledge and ramps that are used in every "I Can" class session. These resources provide and safe and realistic skateboarding experience to students as they hone their skills on obstacles that they would find in an outdoor skatepark. Thank you, Keen Ramps!

    Indo Board Balance Boards

    Indo Board has kindly donated balance boards to the "I Can" Program, a vital training tool that has benefitted students by strengthening their balance skills and board control. Thank you, Indo Board!

    Thank you!

    To all of our partners, we thank you for your generous donations of supplies, resources, prizes, and your time. Every donation we receive allows us to continue to develop youth in the underserved areas of Toledo through skateboarding lessons, and we are so grateful for your contributions to our mission. We can't wait to see what comes next and who we are lucky to add to our partnerships list in the future. A huge THANK YOU from everyone at On-Deck Alliance!
